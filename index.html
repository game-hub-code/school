Mount St. Patrick Academy

With a majestic mountain for a background, as the very name indicates, Mount St. Patrick Academy — one of the latest educational endeavours of the Patrician Brothers in India — is located in Lohagaon, Pune.

We stand poised on the threshold of a new era in education — one of renaissance and change — while holding firmly to the values of discipline, compassion and community that the Patrician tradition is built on.
